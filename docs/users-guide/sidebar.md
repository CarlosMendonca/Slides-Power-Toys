# Interactive Sidebar

In addition to the full **Extensions > Slides Power Toys** menu bar, you can open the persistent sidebar via **Extensions > Slides Power Toys > Open sidebar**.

The sidebar stays docked alongside your presentation for rapid iterative edits without reopening menus, and adds controls not available in fixed menu items:

- **Layout tab**
  - **Precision Snap** with selectable units (`in`, `cm`, `pt`) and decimal precision (`0`–`2`)
  - **Copy Attributes** (Width, Height, Width & Height, Rotation)
  - **Adjoin** with custom gap/padding and optional anchor centering
  - **Align** (Inner / Outer edge toggle + Center on Page) and **Flip**
- **Color tab**
  - **Live Color Inspector** with checkerboard transparency preview, RGB, Hex, HSL, LCH, and Theme color badge
  - **Quick actions**: Swap Text/Fill, Invert Fill, Max Contrast
  - **Interactive sliders & step buttons** for Hue (with quick swatches), Saturation, Luminosity, and Transparency
